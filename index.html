How a job moves

From brief to your door.

Five stages. One team. Every job is proofed before it prints and checked before it leaves.

  2. Stage 01

    Brief

    Send your design, sketch or idea on WhatsApp, email or the quote form.

    Input: PDF · AI · PSD · JPG · PNG
  3. Stage 02

    Design

    We prepare artwork, set bleed and colour, and send a proof for your approval.

    Proof: digital · approved before print
  4. Stage 03

    Print

    Digital, offset, large-format, sublimation or PVC — matched to the job.

    Colour: CMYK · spot on request
  5. Stage 04

    Finish

    Cutting, laminating, binding, mounting and a final quality check.

    Check: every job before dispatch
  6. Stage 05

    Deliver

    Collect from Nasser Road or we deliver across Kampala and the regions.

    Route: Kampala · Central · all regions
